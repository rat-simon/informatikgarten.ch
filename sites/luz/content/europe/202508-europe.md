**Orientierungslosigkeit** (noun, feminine)

*Pronunciation*: /ɔʁiɛnˈtiːʁʊŋsloːzɪçkaɪ̯t/

**Etymology**: From German Orientierung (orientation) + -losigkeit (suffix expressing lack or absence of something)

**Definition**:
1. A state of lacking orientation or direction; the condition of being without bearings or guidance
2. A profound sense of being lost, confused, or uncertain about one's position, purpose, or circumstances
3. Political/Social context: The feeling of uncertainty about one's beliefs, values, or place within political or social systems

I feel lost in a world of where politics is dominated by superficiality and entertainment value. I long for politicians who have thought deeply about the issues they are dealing with, who have a clear vision of what they want to achieve and how they want to get there. I want politicians who are not afraid to take a stand, who are not afraid to be unpopular, who are not afraid to be honest. I want politicians who are not afraid to be themselves.
